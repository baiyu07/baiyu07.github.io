10 Best Pandabuy Alternatives in 2026: Ranked by Category

TL;DR — Key Takeaways

  • Pandabuy's shutdown left its users choosing a new agent without a map. This guide ranks 10 alternatives by category — not one overall winner, but the right pick for each kind of buyer.
  • Best overall: CFans — 1688 and Taobao purchasing, 3–5 free QC photos, 60 days of free storage, and a published 8% of goods plus 8% of international freight.
  • Do not pick from a headline fee alone. Match the category to your order: beginners, EU buyers, fashion finds, large hauls and second-hand all point to different agents.
  • Competitor terms below come from each agent's own fee page or guide, checked in October 2026. Where a term is not published, the guide says so — confirm before you pay.

When Pandabuy shut down, its buyers did not share one need — some wanted the cheapest fee, others wanted fashion QC, others wanted a European route or a beginner-friendly flow. A single 1-to-10 ranking hides those differences, so this guide does the opposite: ten alternatives, each ranked first in exactly one category. Find your category, then compare its pick against your actual order. This is a category ranking, not the cost, QC and switching framework of the earlier Pandabuy alternatives guide — for the migration checklist, read that guide alongside this one.

1. Best Overall: CFans

CFans takes the overall pick on control, not on headline fee. It purchases from Taobao, 1688 and other supported platforms, completes the purchase within 24 hours after payment, includes 3–5 free QC photos per item, gives 60 days of free storage, and charges a published 8% of goods plus 8% of international freight set out in its service agreement. For a buyer moving a whole Pandabuy haul — mixed sellers, mixed categories, photos before dispatch — that combination is the safest default. It is not the cheapest headline fee; it is the most predictable total.

2. Best for Beginners: Kakobuy

Community comparison guides consistently point first-time buyers toward Kakobuy, and spreadsheet guides cite its fee range around 0–3%. Treat both as guide positioning, not verified terms — confirm Kakobuy's current fee page and QC terms at checkout before ordering (verify live). The category logic stands regardless: beginners benefit most from a simple flow and a community that has already documented it.

3. Best for Lowest Headline Fee: Superbuy

Superbuy publishes 0% standard purchasing fees on Taobao, Tmall, JD.com and 1688, with 90 days of free storage (then CNY 0.1 per item per day). Detailed photos cost CNY 3, and Xianyu orders carry a flat CNY 20 per item. If your orders are straightforward, standard-platform purchases and you need little beyond the basics, the purchasing line genuinely is zero — but price the whole checkout, including freight and payment charges, before calling it cheapest.

4. Best for Fashion and Weidian Finds: Fishgoo

Fishgoo's own Weidian guide describes its service fee as 0%, with 5 free QC photos per item and 2,000+ shipping routes. For fashion and sneaker buyers migrating from Pandabuy — the category where seller vetting and QC photos decide everything — that is a strong combination on paper. Verify the current terms at checkout, and judge it on the same full-checkout basis as every other pick here.

5. Best for Seller Questions: CSSBuy

CSSBuy documents a Contact Seller service for investigating product questions, alongside purchasing and warehouse checks. Its published fee is 6% of product price plus China freight, with 90 days of free storage and 3 to 5 QC photos. When the listing is unclear — a strap that may be optional, two materially different sizes, accessories that may not be included — the ability to get an answer before purchase is worth more than a lower fee.

6. Best for Large Hauls: Sugargoo

Sugargoo charges 0% on standard purchases with a CNY 8 standard packing fee per parcel, and gives 100 days of free storage for purchases. For buyers consolidating many items into one parcel over weeks, the long storage window and fixed packing charge spread thinly across the haul. Its guides also walk first-time buyers through DIY orders, which makes it a reasonable second pick for beginners.

7. Best for PayPal Checkout: Mulebuy

Mulebuy's blog states 0% on standard purchases with 90 days of free storage, and it accepts PayPal in USD and EUR. For buyers who want PayPal at both the purchase and shipping stages, that payment coverage is the deciding feature — but check whether your order counts as "standard" before you pay, and confirm its current QC terms (verify live).

8. Best for EU Buyers: ACBuy

Community comparison guides position ACBuy as a Europe-focused option for migrating Pandabuy buyers. Treat that as guide positioning and confirm its current EU routes, fees and QC terms at checkout (verify live). The category matters because EU buyers face VAT handling and route availability that a general ranking ignores.

9. Best for French-Speaking Buyers: Parcel Up

Parcel Up explicitly describes French-language shopping assistance and lists CAD payment support. Its published service fee is 10% of the item price including domestic delivery, with a minimum of RMB 35 — on a small item, the minimum can exceed the percentage, so work it out for your basket. For French-speaking buyers, language support is a real selection factor, not a bonus feature.

10. Best for Second-Hand (Xianyu): Jiyun

Jiyun runs a dedicated Xianyu proxy service at a flat 15% of product value, per its product page, with seller verification and inspection included. Second-hand is a different job from new goods — condition checks, seller negotiation and realistic expectations — and a dedicated proxy fits it better than a general agent's standard flow. Confirm photo counts and storage terms before ordering (verify live).

How to Use This Ranking Without Misusing It

Three rules keep a category ranking honest. First, find your category before you compare prices — a beginner who picks the lowest-fee agent and an EU buyer who picks the fashion agent have both misread the guide. Second, run your real parcel through two finalists' checkouts: same items, same postcode, same packing, same treatment of taxes. Third, treat any term marked verify live as unconfirmed until you have seen it on the agent's own page. Older comparison names such as Basetao and Wegobuy also appear in Pandabuy-era guides; their current terms were not re-checked for this article, so compare them the same way if you shortlist them.
