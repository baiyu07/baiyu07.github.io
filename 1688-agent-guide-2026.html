Part 1 · Understand the marketplace

What is 1688, and why can its prices be lower?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's domestic Chinese business-to-business wholesale marketplace.
It connects Chinese factories, wholesalers, distributors and brand owners with buyers who usually purchase for resale or production. Listings commonly use quantity-based price tiers: as the order quantity rises, the unit price falls. Unlike export-first marketplaces, 1688 is designed around Chinese-language negotiation, renminbi payment and delivery to a Chinese address. [1][2]

The low price is a business model, not a magic discount

Many 1688 listings are close to the manufacturing or primary-distribution layer. The seller may spend less on English sales staff, export packaging, international advertising and cross-border support. Wholesale price tiers also reward buyers who accept a larger minimum order quantity (MOQ). That can create a real unit-cost advantage, but only when the product, specification and quantity are genuinely comparable.

Do not compare the biggest-font price on one listing with a Taobao checkout price. A 1688 price range may show the lowest tier reserved for a larger quantity, a simpler specification or an unfinished product. Compare the same material, dimensions, accessories, packaging and order size.

What “factory-direct” should mean in practice

Treat “factory direct” as a hypothesis to verify, not a promise. Ask who makes the item, whether production is in-house, whether the quoted price includes your exact material and packaging, and how the lead time changes at your quantity. On 1688, the company profile may identify the operating model and show factory records, while listing-level evidence shows current sales, delivery and buyer feedback. [1]

CFans verdict:
1688 is most valuable when the purchase is repeatable. The more confidently you can specify the same item and reorder it, the more likely wholesale pricing will outweigh the platform's extra friction.

One important distinction: 1688 is not Alibaba.com

Alibaba.com is built for global B2B trade; 1688 is built primarily for China's domestic market. Overseas buyers can access 1688, and some direct cross-border payment paths now exist for eligible buyers, but most sellers still work in Chinese and expect domestic fulfillment. A buying agent packages those disconnected steps into one order flow. [1]

Part 2 · Remove the barriers

Why is buying directly difficult for a foreigner?

The barriers are operational. Each one maps to a specific job a capable agent should perform.

The interface and product data are Chinese-first

Machine translation can reveal the category and headline, but it can miss material grades, size conventions, production tolerances and variant notes. An agent should confirm the exact specification with the seller, not merely translate the page.

Translate the buying decision

CFans lets the buyer submit a product link and instructions. The useful output is a confirmed variant, quantity, price and seller answer - not a word-for-word translation.

Payment is built around Chinese methods

1688 primarily uses Alipay, WeChat Pay and mainland bank transfers. Some sellers or eligible overseas buyers may have other options, but availability varies. WorldFirst describes World Pay as an authorized international payment route for participating 1688 suppliers. [1]

Collect overseas payment and pay locally

The agent receives the buyer’s payment through its supported methods, converts funds and completes the domestic purchase. The buyer must still check the exchange rate, processing fee and refund rules. CFans supports Credit Card (two channels) and PayPal, with a minimum top-up of CNY 6.10 per channel, quick amounts of ¥500/¥1000/¥2000 or a custom amount; funds credit 1–120 minutes after a successful top-up, withdrawals take 10–15 business days and refunds 10–25 business days. Add a billing address before paying and do not use a VPN or proxy while paying (cfans.com, Sep 2026).

MOQ and price tiers do not match a test order

The headline price may require dozens or hundreds of units. Factories may also set a minimum spend, a minimum per color, or a separate MOQ for custom logos.

Ask for a workable small order

An agent can ask whether colors or sizes may be mixed, whether a paid sample is possible, or whether the seller supports below-MOQ purchasing. An agent's aggregate volume may help, but it cannot guarantee that every seller will split an MOQ.

Sellers usually deliver inside China

Most suppliers quote domestic delivery to a Chinese address, not door-to-door export. International freight, customs data and destination restrictions are separate decisions. [1]

Receive, inspect, consolidate and export

Goods go to the agent's warehouse. After quality-control (QC) photos and any issue resolution, the buyer combines parcels, selects a route and submits the international shipment.

Do not overgeneralize the payment barrier.
It is no longer accurate to say that every foreign buyer must have a Chinese bank account. Direct options exist for some eligible buyers and suppliers. The agent route remains attractive because it combines payment with Chinese communication, warehouse handling, QC and export shipping.

      The lowest unit price can produce the highest landed cost

      The CFans True Total Cost formula is a decision framework for comparing agents and marketplaces on the full cost of a usable item, not the checkout headline.

      True Total Cost = product price + China domestic freight + agent service fee + foreign-exchange spread + optional inspection/services + packing + international freight + duty/tax + expected defect loss

      Use the formula per usable unit

      Suppose a batch costs ¥1,000 before shipping. CFans’ 0% purchasing fee would add ¥0 (basic purchasing service is free, cfans.com, Sep 2026), but that is only one line. Add seller-to-warehouse delivery, the exchange rate actually charged, export packing, chargeable international weight and destination taxes. Then estimate defect loss: if a 100-unit order leaves 95 sellable units, divide the full landed total by 95, not 100.

      Do not invent a universal “1688 is X% cheaper” claim. The saving depends on product parity, quantity tier and route. The defensible test is whether the same-spec, landed, usable-unit cost beats the Taobao or local alternative.

      Part 8 · Put the guide into action

      Is 1688 legit and safe?

      Yes, 1688 is a legitimate Alibaba Group wholesale marketplace, but every seller and product still needs vetting. According to CFans' recommended process, buyers should combine seller identity and transaction signals with a sample and warehouse QC; no badge alone guarantees quality.

      Can I buy just one item on 1688?

      Sometimes. Some ready-stock listings or sellers allow a one-piece sample at a higher unit price, while factories may require a larger MOQ. CFans can ask whether a seller will accept a sample, mixed variants or a below-MOQ order, but seller approval is not guaranteed.

      Why is 1688 cheaper than Taobao?

      1688 often prices for wholesale quantities and connects buyers with factories, wholesalers and distributors; Taobao usually prices for retail purchases. CFans recommends comparing the same specification and quantity, then applying the True Total Cost formula before calling one option cheaper.

      Do I need to speak Chinese to buy from 1688?

      No, not when you use a capable 1688 agent. CFans can handle purchasing and seller communication in the domestic workflow. You still need to provide precise product requirements so the agent knows what to confirm.

      How do foreigners pay on 1688?

      The simplest integrated route is usually to pay through an agent, which then completes the domestic purchase. Eligible direct buyers may also use supported international-card options or an authorized cross-border payment service for participating suppliers. CFans' checkout methods, currency conversion and current fees should be checked live before payment. [1]

      What if the quality is bad?

      Stop the order before international shipping and document the mismatch. Use CFans QC photos, your acceptance sheet and added evidence to request exchange, return or another remedy while the goods remain in China. The seller's policy, timing and proof requirements determine the outcome; international returns are usually harder and more expensive. CFans’ return/exchange window is 7 days after signing or 5 days after warehouse arrival; a domestic return costs the return shipping plus a return service fee (amount not published by CFans)

      What does a 1688 agent not replace?

      An agent does not replace product compliance advice, laboratory testing, trademark authorization, destination-country customs rules, liability insurance or your own margin analysis. For safety-critical, regulated or high-value goods, commission the appropriate professional inspection and legal or customs review.
