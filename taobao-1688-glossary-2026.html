Taobao & 1688 Glossary for Foreign Buyers (2026)

Shopping on Taobao or 1688 as a foreign buyer means running into Chinese logistics terms on every screen: 合箱 (consolidation), 体积重 (volumetric weight), 补差价 (price-difference top-up), 清关 (customs clearance). Misunderstanding one of them can quietly change your bill. This glossary explains each term in plain English, shows the Chinese original so you can match it to what you see on the page, and — most importantly — tells you why you should care as the person paying.

Ordering & payment terms

Buying agent （代购 / 买手）

A company in China that buys from Taobao or 1688 on your behalf, receives the goods at a local warehouse, checks them, and ships internationally. You shop in your currency with an international payment method; the agent handles Chinese payment, domestic delivery, consolidation, and export shipping.

Why you care: Most Taobao sellers ship only within mainland China and most do not accept foreign cards or communicate in English. A buying agent is the practical workaround for all three barriers at once — language, payment, and domestic-only delivery. (Do you need a Taobao agent?)

MOQ (minimum order quantity) （起订量）

The smallest number of units a seller will sell in one order. Common on 1688, which is a wholesale marketplace; many listings show "5 件起批" (5 pieces minimum).

Why you care: MOQ decides which platform fits your order. If you need one piece, a Taobao retail listing with no MOQ is cheaper than forcing a wholesale deal. If you need dozens, 1688's MOQ structure is usually where the lower unit price lives.

SKU (stock keeping unit) （库存单位 / 商品编码）

A unique code for each exact variant of a product — size, color, and model combination. When an agent asks you to confirm the SKU, they want you to double-check you picked the right variant, because warehouse staff can only verify what the SKU says.

Why you care: Most "wrong item received" cases are actually wrong-SKU orders. Re-reading the SKU at checkout is the cheapest quality control step that exists.

Price-difference top-up （补差价）

When the item price moves between the time you place the order and the time the agent actually purchases from the seller, the agent asks you to cover the gap. CFans only asks for a top-up when the difference exceeds min(2% of the item total, ¥10) — and charges no handling fee on top-ups: the money goes straight to the seller.

Why you care: This is a trust signal. A clear threshold means you are never asked for small unexplained payments, and "no handling fee" means the top-up is not a hidden revenue line.

Wallet top-up （充值）

Adding funds to your account balance before the agent can pay sellers. CFans accepts credit card (two channels) and PayPal, with a minimum top-up of CNY 6.10 per channel; funds credit within 1–120 minutes of a successful top-up. Add a billing address first, and do not use a VPN or proxy during payment.

Why you care: You pay twice on an agent order — once for the goods (wallet top-up), once later for international freight. Knowing the minimum and the credit time helps you plan when to fund the account.

QC & warehouse terms

QC inspection （验货 / 质检）

The warehouse check of your items after domestic arrival: screening for prohibited items and verifying style, quantity, color, size, model, damage, stains, and defects, with photos you can review before shipment. CFans' standard inspection includes 3–5 photos per item depending on the product category at no separate fee; visible defects are flagged at ≥0.5 cm in diameter or ≥1 cm in length. Paid upgrades exist: HD custom photos at 1.5 RMB/photo (delivered within 24 hours) and video QC at 35 RMB/item (20–90 seconds).

Why you care: QC photos are your only chance to catch a wrong or damaged item while it is still in China — fixing it there is far cheaper than a cross-border return. Read the photos as risk reduction, not as a guarantee of hidden quality or authenticity. (QC photos explained)

Warehouse check-in （入库 / 签收）

The step where the warehouse receives your parcel from the domestic courier, registers it to your account, and runs the QC inspection. CFans completes warehouse check-in QC within 3–5 business days of domestic arrival, and the agent's purchase from the seller is completed within 24 hours after your payment.

Why you care: These two timers start your whole timeline. If check-in is slow, your consolidated shipment waits — while storage days keep counting.

Warehousing / storage （仓储）

Your goods sit at the agent's warehouse while you finish shopping, so multiple orders from different sellers can be combined into one international parcel. CFans offers 60 days of free storage from the "Warehouse In" status; you can renew for 30 more days at 10 RMB via the Renew button (renewal available from 20 days before expiry until 7 days after). Parcels left unprocessed past the deadline are treated as abandoned and disposed of.

Why you care: Free storage is what makes consolidation affordable — you can buy over several weeks and ship once. Set your own deadline well before day 60.

Shipping & logistics terms

Domestic shipping （国内段）

The first leg: from the seller to the agent's warehouse in China. This is usually cheap or free (many Taobao sellers offer free domestic shipping), and it is paid or confirmed at order time — separate from the international freight you pay later.

Why you care: International freight is normally billed only after the warehouse weighs and measures your packed parcel. Budget the two legs separately.

Consolidation （合箱）

Combining several warehouse parcels — possibly from different sellers on Taobao and 1688 — into one international shipment, with packaging optimized to cut volume.

Why you care: This is the single biggest lever on your shipping bill. One international parcel means one base charge and one customs declaration instead of many. Almost every saving strategy on an agent platform starts here.

Volumetric (dimensional) weight （体积重）

A billed weight calculated from the parcel's dimensions, used when a parcel is light but bulky. The rule on CFans lines: volumetric weight = L × W × H ÷ 6000 on the USA and UK lines, ÷ 8000 on the Germany line (length × width × height in cm). You are charged on whichever is greater — actual or volumetric weight.

Why you care: A plush toy can cost more to ship than a cast-iron pan of the same scale weight. Consolidation and repacking shrink the box; shrinking the box shrinks the volumetric weight.

Parcel forwarding （转运）

Moving your already-purchased goods from one country to another — in agent terms, forwarding your consolidated warehouse parcel to your doorstep abroad. It is the export leg of the journey, handled by shipping lines with defined weight/size limits and transit windows.

Why you care: "Forwarding" is the part you shop for: compare lines by estimated transit time, restrictions (batteries, liquids, branded goods), and what the example quotes actually cover.

Direct shipping （直邮）

When a seller or platform ships straight to your overseas address without an agent's warehouse in the middle. Some Taobao sellers offer official consolidated shipping （官方集运） variants of this, but many sellers simply do not ship abroad at all.

Why you care: Direct shipping removes the warehouse step — and with it, QC photos, consolidation, and repacking. It is simpler for one small item; it is usually worse value for multi-item orders.

Sensitive goods （敏感货）

Items that face extra rules on international lines: cosmetics, liquids, powders, batteries, magnets, food, and branded goods. They typically need a "sensitive" or "special" shipping line with different pricing and restrictions. CFans' example 1000g quotes (Sep 2026) are on such lines: USA line ¥250.85 / 8–12 days, UK line ¥162.40 / 9–12 days, Germany line ¥171.10 / 8–13 days — example quotes vary ±5–10% and by weight/line, not a fixed price list.

Why you care: Check whether your items are "sensitive" before buying. A line that rejects your goods at the warehouse is a dead end; choosing the right line upfront saves a return.

Customs clearance （清关）

The export inspection in China and import inspection in your destination country. The agent's warehouse consolidates your items into one declaration; duties, taxes, and admissibility are normally the recipient's responsibility and depend on your country's rules.

Why you care: One consolidated parcel means one clearance — simpler and often cheaper than many small ones. Declare truthfully: CFans' Help Center advises declaring at least 30% of the total goods value, max USD 135 (and max CAD 20 for Canada).

Declared value （申报价值）

The value written on the customs declaration for your parcel. It affects duties and is also the basis for insurance claims. CFans offers two insurance types — customs-seizure insurance and parcel loss/damage insurance — purchased when you select the shipping line (some lines include it free); the payout equals actual shipping plus actual goods value, up to the insured amount. Claim within 7 days of signing or 45 days of shipping; insurance prices are not published, so confirm the live price.

Why you care: Declared value links customs and insurance together. Under-declare and a claim pays less; over-declare and you may pay more duty. Follow the destination guidance rather than guessing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Taobao buying agent?

A Taobao buying agent is a company in China that purchases from Taobao on your behalf, receives the goods at a local warehouse, provides QC inspection photos, stores and consolidates your orders, and arranges international shipping. It solves the three main barriers for foreign buyers: domestic-only sellers, Chinese payment methods, and the language gap.

Do I need a Taobao agent to buy from Taobao?

Not always. If a seller ships directly to your country and you can pay and read the listing, you can order directly. An agent becomes worth it when sellers ship only within China, when you order from several stores (so you can consolidate into one parcel), or when you want QC photos before international shipment. See Do you need a Taobao agent? for the full decision guide.

What is parcel consolidation （合箱）?

Parcel consolidation is the warehouse service of combining multiple parcels from different sellers into one international shipment, with packaging optimized to reduce volume. It is the main reason agent shipping can beat direct shipping for multi-item orders: one base charge, one customs declaration, one tracking number.

How is volumetric weight calculated?

Volumetric (dimensional) weight is calculated from the parcel's dimensions: length × width × height divided by a line-specific divisor. On CFans lines it is L×W×H÷6000 for the USA and UK lines and ÷8000 for the Germany line. You pay based on whichever is greater — actual weight or volumetric weight — so repacking a bulky parcel can lower the bill.

What counts as sensitive goods?

Sensitive goods （敏感货） are items with extra rules on international lines: cosmetics, liquids, powders, batteries, magnets, food, and branded goods. They usually need a dedicated "sensitive" or "special" shipping line. Check your items against the line's eligibility list before buying, because a rejected parcel at the warehouse costs you a domestic return.

How long does warehouse QC take with CFans?

CFans completes warehouse check-in QC within 3–5 business days of domestic arrival, and purchases from sellers are completed within 24 hours after your payment. Storage is free for 60 days from the "Warehouse In" status, renewable for 30 days at 10 RMB via the Renew button. See QC photos explained for how to read the inspection photos.

What happens if my parcel stays in the warehouse too long?

On CFans, parcels left unprocessed after the 60-day free storage period (without a paid renewal) are treated as abandoned and disposed of. Reminder emails go out before and after expiry — but set your own ship-by date earlier to be safe.
