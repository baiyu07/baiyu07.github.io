Direct answer first: Xianyu does not ship internationally, and neither does Goofish — because they are the same marketplace. Xianyu (闲鱼) is Alibaba's second-hand platform; Goofish is simply its name for international users. A seller link from either name points to the same listing, and neither version offers international checkout, foreign payment or overseas delivery. If you found a second-hand treasure there, the way to get it abroad is a buying agent — and on second-hand goods, the agent's condition check is the most important part of the whole purchase.

Why Xianyu Cannot Ship Abroad Directly

  • Private sellers, not shops. Listings come from individuals clearing out their own items. They have no export setup, no international shipping workflow, and no reason to build one for a single sale.
  • Domestic logistics only. Sellers ship to Chinese addresses and are paid through Chinese payment methods.
  • Chinese-only communication. Condition questions, negotiation and delivery follow-ups all happen in Chinese.
  • No buyer protection for overseas buyers. Without a local account and address, you have no practical recourse if the item arrives different from the listing.

Shipping Second-Hand: Three Extra Checks

Shipping a used item adds questions that new goods never raise:

  • Condition before freight. International shipping is the most expensive and least reversible part of the order. Never pay it on a description — pay it on QC photos you have approved.
  • Battery and electronics rules. Used electronics with batteries can face carrier restrictions. Confirm the carrier accepts your item before the parcel ships, not after.
  • After-sale coverage differs for used items. Agents' return and dispute terms for second-hand goods are not the same as for new goods. Confirm what happens if the item arrives materially different — before you order.

How Long Does Xianyu Shipping Take?

There is no single published transit time for second-hand orders, because the seller leg varies: a responsive private seller ships in days; a slow one takes longer, and the agent can only follow up, not force a shipment. After warehouse intake, the timeline looks like any other agent order: QC review, consolidation, then the international leg you choose. Budget two to four weeks end to end for a typical order, and treat any faster promise as a guess, not a term.

FAQ

Does Xianyu ship to the US?

No. Xianyu does not ship internationally. US buyers order through a buying agent that purchases in China and ships the item to the US.

Does Goofish ship internationally?

No — Goofish is Xianyu's international name, and it is the same marketplace with the same domestic-only shipping. The agent route is identical.

Can I track my Xianyu order abroad?

Yes. Once the parcel ships internationally, the agent provides tracking for the international leg. The domestic seller-to-warehouse leg is tracked inside the agent's order page.

What if the second-hand item arrives different from the listing?

That is what warehouse QC is for: catch it before international shipping, while an exchange or return is still practical. Confirm the agent's second-hand after-sale terms before you order.
