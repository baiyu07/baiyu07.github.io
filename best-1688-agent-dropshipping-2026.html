Dropshipping from 1688 promises what Taobao cannot: wholesale unit prices on products you never hold in stock. The catch is that 1688 was built for Chinese businesses buying in bulk, not for overseas sellers shipping single orders to end customers. Listings are Chinese-only, suppliers expect Chinese payment and domestic addresses, and minimum order quantities vary by listing. A 1688 agent bridges all of that — and this guide explains which agent fits dropshipping, and how to run the model without the usual first-month failures.

Why 1688 for Dropshipping — and Why It Needs an Agent

1688's advantage is price: the same product that retails on Taobao often wholesales cheaper on 1688, which is the margin a dropshipping store lives on. Its complications are structural:

  • Wholesale logic. Many listings carry minimum order quantities or price breaks by quantity. A single-unit test order may cost more per unit, or may not be accepted at all — check the listing before building a product page around it.
  • Chinese-only everything. Listings, supplier chat, specifications and after-sale terms are in Chinese.
  • Domestic logistics. Suppliers ship to Chinese addresses and expect Chinese payment.
  • Quality variance. Wholesale suppliers vary more than retail sellers. A sample order is not optional — it is the business model.

An agent converts that wholesale marketplace into a dropshipping supply chain: it buys from the supplier, receives goods at its warehouse, photographs them, and ships to your customer or to you for onward dispatch, depending on the arrangement you confirm.

How to Run 1688 Dropshipping with an Agent

  1. Find suppliers, then verify them. Shortlist listings with clear specifications and responsive suppliers. Check minimum quantities and price breaks before you list anything.
  2. Order samples first. Buy one unit through the agent, review the 3–5 free QC photos, and check the real product against the listing — size, material, packaging, defects. A supplier that fails the sample never reaches your store.
  3. Confirm dispatch options in writing. Before scaling, establish how orders ship to end customers, what packaging is used, what tracking is provided, and how returns are handled. Get each answer for your actual orders, not as a general promise.
  4. Price from the full chain. Goods + domestic delivery + agent fee + international freight + payment charges + any return reserve. A product that looks profitable on goods alone usually is not.
  5. Scale one supplier at a time. Add volume to a supplier that has already passed samples and dispatch tests. New suppliers restart the sample loop.

What to Check Before Choosing a 1688 Dropshipping Agent

  • Sample economics. What does one unit really cost delivered, including fees and freight? If sampling is uneconomical, the supplier pipeline is broken.
  • QC depth. Are photos free, and do they show what your customers will complain about — sizing, materials, defects, packaging?
  • Storage clock. How long can stock sit free while you test demand? CFans gives 60 days; others range from 90 to 100 days — match the clock to your test cycle.
  • Dispatch control. Can you control packaging, tracking and delivery speed for end customers? Confirm, don't assume.
  • Returns reality. Who pays for a return, and how does a defective unit get replaced? Write it down before your first customer order.

FAQ

Do I need inventory to dropship from 1688?

No — that is the point of the model. You list products, customers order, and the agent purchases and ships per order. Samples are the exception: always hold and inspect samples before listing, even though you hold no customer stock.

Can I dropship from 1688 to the US and Europe?

Yes, through an agent's international shipping lines. Confirm the line serves your customers' postcodes, what tracking it provides, and how customs and VAT are handled for each destination before scaling.

What about minimum order quantities on 1688?

MOQs vary by listing and supplier — some accept single units, others do not. Check the listing and confirm with the supplier through the agent before building a product page around an item.

How do returns work for 1688 dropshipping?

It depends on the agent's terms and the supplier's policy. Establish who pays return freight, what the deadlines are, and how defective units are replaced — before your first customer order, not after the first complaint.

Is 1688 better than Taobao for dropshipping?

For margin, usually yes — wholesale prices beat retail. For simplicity, no — 1688 adds supplier minimums, wholesale variance and a Chinese-only workflow. Taobao suits testing; 1688 suits scaling a proven product.
