## From product link to international shipment

CFANS is a China cross-border shopping platform that helps overseas buyers purchase from Chinese marketplaces and arrange delivery. Its service connects product sourcing and ordering with warehouse inspection, storage, repacking, consolidation and international shipping arrangements. See [About CFANS](https://www.cfans.com/content/about) for the platform's service overview.

The [CFANS Help Center](https://www.cfans.com/content/help) lists automatic product parsing for **Taobao, Tmall, 1688 and Weidian**. Start with a product link, confirm the variant and quantity, and review the purchasing order. For a link that does not parse, [DIY Orders](https://www.cfans.com/fulfillment/diy-orders) provides a manual request route.

After the seller sends goods to the warehouse, review the available inspection information before selecting items for international delivery. You can plan a combined parcel or separate shipments. The available methods depend on the destination, item type, weight and dimensions.

The [beginner guide](https://www.cfans.com/content/beginner-guide) explains the current ordering and shipping steps.

## Build a useful shipping estimate

Prepare four details before using the estimator:

- **Destination:** the country or region where the parcel will be delivered.
- **Item type:** describe the actual goods so you can check route restrictions.
- **Packed weight and dimensions:** packaging affects the final parcel, and some routes use volumetric weight.
- **Packaging and service needs:** decide whether original boxes, protective packing or separate parcels matter for the order.

Compare the complete purchase-and-delivery budget, including product price, domestic shipping, applicable service and payment charges, international freight and any destination charges not already included. A warehouse QC check helps identify visible issues within its inspection scope; it is not authentication or functional testing. Consolidation can change both the cost and route eligibility, so compare the resulting parcel before choosing it.

**Next step: [estimate your parcel's shipping options on CFANS](https://www.cfans.com/fulfillment/estimation).** Use the current quote and service terms when making the purchase decision; dated guide examples provide context.
