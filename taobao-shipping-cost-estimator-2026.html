FAQ

How much does Taobao shipping cost?

There is no flat rate — shipping is charged by chargeable weight (actual vs. volumetric, whichever is greater) on the line you choose. As a verified reference point: CFans' September 2026 logged-in quotes at 1000g were USA ¥250.85 (8–12 days), UK ¥162.40 (9–12 days), Germany ¥171.10 (8–13 days), floating ±5–10% in reality. For the verified CFans USA line: 8–12 days after dispatch. Add 3–5 business days of domestic warehouse QC after your Taobao parcels arrive at CFans' warehouse, plus purchase time (CFans' SLA: purchase within 24 hours after payment). Customs clearance time at the destination varies and isn't something any agent can guarantee.

Will I pay tariffs or customs charges on a Taobao order shipped to the US?

The old US$800 duty-free de minimis channel for small parcels has been suspended globally effective 29 August 2025 (Executive Order 14324), and the suspension was made indefinite with a rule published in the Federal Register on 24 June 2026 — so assume your parcel enters as a declared, dutiable shipment. What you actually pay is decided by U.S. Customs based on declared value and classification; we won't invent a percentage. Official sources: White House (E.O. 14324 continuation), Federal Register, 24 June 2026. CFans advises declaring at least 30% of the goods' value (max USD 135) per its Help Center.
