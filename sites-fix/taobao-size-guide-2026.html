Why Taobao sizes "run small" — and why the label alone means little

The common advice "size up on Taobao" is only half the story. Chinese domestic brands design for different body proportions: narrower shoulders, shorter torso and sleeve lengths, and closer-fitting cuts. A US Medium and an Asian "L" can share a letter on the tag while fitting completely differently.

Three things trip up first-time buyers:

  1. No universal standard. Two Taobao stores selling near-identical hoodies can use completely different size tables. Treat every listing as its own case.
  2. Garment size vs. body size. Many charts list the measurements of the garment itself, not the body it fits. A chest entry of "58 cm" may be a flat half-measurement — doubled, that is 116 cm around. Always check whether the chart shows flat (half) or full (circumference) measurements; when in doubt, assume flat and double it.
  3. The 号型 (hao-xing) notation. You will see labels like 165/88A on Chinese clothing. The first number is the recommended height in cm (165), the second is the chest or bust in cm (88), and the letter is the body-type code (A = standard). It is a fit hint, not a size you can convert directly.

Bottom line: an "XL" means nothing without numbers. Compare the listing's measurements with your own, not with the size you wear at home.

Bottoms: pants, jeans and skirts

Bottoms cause the most size mistakes because waist and length both matter, and many listings use flat waist measurements. A listing that says "waist 40 cm" usually means 40 cm laid flat — 80 cm around. Double flat measurements before comparing.

For women's bottoms, match your waist and hip circumferences to the chart rather than the S/M/L label, and check the inseam or total length (裤长) — inseams often run shorter, which matters for taller buyers. For jeans, look for whether the listing gives the waist as a flat or full measurement before you decide.

Shoes: use your foot length in millimeters

Shoe sizing is actually the most reliable category to convert, because Chinese shoe sizes are usually based on foot length. Most Taobao shoe listings use EU-style numbers, and many include the foot length in millimeters — that mm number is your anchor. Measure your foot heel-to-toe in the evening (feet swell slightly during the day), in millimeters.

Shoe notes: larger sizes (above EU 44/45) are harder to find on Taobao, and some sellers mark special or non-standard sizes in the description — read it before ordering. If the listing only shows a CN number like "260", that is the foot length in millimeters (260 = 26.0 cm). When your foot measures between sizes, buyers commonly go up half a size; width matters too, so ask the seller for the shoe's width if you have wide feet.

How to measure yourself in 5 minutes

You need a soft measuring tape, a mirror, and fitted clothing or underwear (never measure over bulky layers). Do not pull the tape tight — it should sit flat against the body without compressing.

  1. Chest/bust: wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level across your back.
  2. Waist: find your natural waistline (the narrowest point, usually above the navel) and measure around it.
  3. Hips: stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and buttocks.
  4. Shoulder width: measure from one shoulder bone tip to the other across your back — critical for jackets and blazers.
  5. Sleeve/inseam: for sleeves, measure from the shoulder tip to the wrist; for inseam, from the crotch to the ankle bone.

Write the numbers down in centimeters, since Taobao charts use cm. A widely used buyer practice is to add a small comfort margin — roughly 2–4 cm for a regular fit, more for a loose fit — but treat that as a personal preference, not a sizing rule. Compare your numbers with the listing's chart, not with the letter size.

How to read a Taobao listing's size chart

Size charts are usually images in the product description, sometimes with no translation. The key Chinese terms:

尺码 — size | 衣长 / 前衣长 — garment length | 胸围 — bust/chest (circumference) | 胸宽 — chest width (often a flat half-measurement — if the number looks like a full circumference, the seller may have mislabeled it) | 肩宽 — shoulder width | 袖长 — sleeve length | 腰围 — waist (circumference) | 臀围 — hip (circumference) | 裤长 — pants length | 脚长 — foot length | 手工测量1–3cm误差 — "hand-measured, expect 1–3 cm of error" | 喜欢宽松可拍大一码 — "size up if you like a loose fit" | 大码 — plus size (search this term to find extended-size listings)

Three checks before you click buy: confirm whether measurements are flat or full; confirm the 1–3 cm hand-measurement tolerance is acceptable for fitted items; and if the listing has no chart at all, message the seller with your height, weight and key measurements and ask for a recommended size — sellers answer these questions routinely. Buyer reviews with photos and comments like "runs small" or "fits like US M" are often more reliable than the official chart.

1688 sizing: what wholesale buyers should know

1688 is a wholesale marketplace, and its sizing works a little differently from Taobao retail. The same style is often produced by multiple factories with their own patterns, so two suppliers' "XL" can differ noticeably. Size runs may be incomplete (mixed or broken size assortments are common in wholesale lots), and bulk listings sometimes show a single sample garment's measurements as representative of the whole run — which it may not be.

Practical approach: before placing a bulk order, ask the supplier for the size specification sheet for the actual production batch, not just the retail-style chart on the listing. Confirm how sizes are distributed across the quantity you are buying, and clarify whether the supplier accepts size exchanges within the order. Because you cannot try on wholesale lots in advance, many buyers use their agent's warehouse inspection to check a sample against the agreed measurements before the full parcel ships internationally. Describe size expectations in writing (numbers, not letters) when you submit the order so there is something concrete to check against.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do Taobao sizes run small?

A: Generally yes — Chinese domestic clothing is typically cut 1–2 sizes smaller than US sizing, with narrower shoulders and shorter lengths. But there is no universal standard: every store sets its own chart, so always compare your body measurements with the listing's numbers instead of relying on the label.

Q: What size am I on Taobao?

A: There is no single answer. Measure your chest/bust, waist, hips and shoulder width in centimeters, then match those numbers to each listing's own size chart. As a rough starting point only, many buyers find their US size corresponds to roughly 1–2 sizes up in Asian sizing (e.g., US M ≈ Asian XL) — confirm per listing.

Q: How do I know my Taobao shoe size?

A: Measure your foot heel-to-toe in millimeters in the evening. Most Taobao shoe listings use EU-style numbers and many show the foot length in mm — match your measurement to that number. As a general reference, 260 mm ≈ EU 41 ≈ US men's 8, but always follow the listing's own chart.

Q: Are Taobao size charts accurate?

A: They are a starting point, not a guarantee. Charts are often hand-measured (expect 1–3 cm of tolerance), some show garment rather than body measurements, and sellers occasionally mislabel flat vs. full measurements. Cross-check with buyer reviews and photos, and message the seller with your measurements when the chart is missing or unclear.

Q: Can a buying agent check the size before my order ships internationally?

A: According to CFans, yes — standard quality inspection checks size, quantity, color and other visible details against your order notes, with 3–5 inspection photos per item included. You can also request HD custom photos (1.5 RMB per photo, within 24 hours) for a close-up of the size tag. This check happens at the China warehouse, before you pay for international shipping.
